Dining

What the coast gives

Fish from the Baltic, game and mushrooms from the Darß forest, vegetables from the farms of the Fischland — and cake from our own kitchen in the afternoon.

The restaurant

Regional, seasonal, without fuss

The menu changes with what the season and the suppliers of the region have to offer. In spring it is the herring, in autumn the forest, in winter the slow cooking.

We cook plainly and carefully: ingredients you can recognise, clear flavours, nothing that needs explaining. If you like, every course comes with a recommendation from the cellar — by the glass as well.

  • An evening menu that changes with the season
  • Vegetarian dishes every evening
  • Half board and full board can be added
The shape of the day

From the breakfast garden to the last glass

  1. 7:30

    Breakfast buffet

    Bread from the baker, fish, cheese and cold cuts from the region, fruit, eggs however you like them. Outside in the breakfast garden in fine weather.

  2. 9:00

    Breakfast in your room

    For mornings that should start more slowly: tell us the evening before and breakfast comes to you.

  3. 14:30

    Coffee and cake in the café

    Cake from our own kitchen, changing with the season — the classic end to a walk on the beach.

  4. 18:00

    Evening menu and sea terrace

    Inside in the restaurant or outside on the terrace, for as long as the sun allows. A table for house guests goes without saying.

Fish from the Baltic

Whatever the coastal boats land is on the menu the same day: cod, herring, pike-perch from the lagoon.

From the Darß forest

Game, mushrooms and herbs from the national park on our doorstep — in autumn the strongest season in the kitchen.

Our own bakery

Cakes and tarts are made in the house. Whatever is left is gone by the evening — which is the measure of quality.

Allergies and preferences: tell us when you enquire — vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free or anything else can almost always be arranged, as long as the kitchen knows in advance.
