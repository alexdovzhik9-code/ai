What this website processes

  • Server log files. When a page is requested, the web server records technically necessary data (IP address, time, file requested, browser type). The legal basis is the legitimate interest in secure operation, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Enquiry form. Your details are used solely to answer your enquiry, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. The form opens your own email programme, so the message travels through your own email provider.
  • Local appearance setting. Your choice between the light and dark appearance is stored in your browser's local storage. That value never leaves your device.
  • Fonts. The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which transmits your IP address to Google. To avoid this, the fonts can be self-hosted — see the note below.

What this website does not do

No cookies are set for analytics or advertising, and no tracking services, social media plugins, map services or advertising networks are embedded.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. The competent authority is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Mecklenburg-Vorpommern.

Note for the operator: to send no data to Google, download the two font families once, place them under assets/fonts/ and replace the Google Fonts link in the page head with local @font-face rules. The rest of the site works unchanged.
