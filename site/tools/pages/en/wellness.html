Spa

Two areas, open to everyone

The pool with its view into the green in Haus Fischerwiege; steam bath, tub and treatments in Haus Namenlos. Your room key works in both.

Haus Fischerwiege

Swimming into the green

The pool lies behind a tall window front onto the garden. You swim your lengths and see nothing but trees — in winter the best place in the house.

  • Indoor pool with a window front onto the garden
  • Several saunas
  • Quiet areas with loungers
Haus Namenlos

Warmth, water, quiet

The second area is smaller and stiller — meant for the hour between a walk on the beach and dinner.

Steam bath

Moist warmth, particularly welcome after cold days by the water.

Tub for two

A whirl tub under a starry sky — reserved for the two of you by appointment.

Solarium

For the days when the Baltic is stingy with its light.

Massage

Treatments and an aquajet lounger in rooms of their own, by appointment.
