Getting here

Ways to reach the hotel
By car Via the A20, exit towards Ribnitz-Damgarten, then onto the Fischland peninsula. Garage at the house.
By train To Ribnitz-Damgarten or Rostock, then by bus via Wustrow to Ahrenshoop.
Once here Beach, galleries, art museum and the village centre are all walkable — the car can stay parked.
