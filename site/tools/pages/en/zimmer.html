Staying

Rooms, suites, apartments

Comfortable country furnishing, equipment for today — and nearly always a balcony or a terrace into the green.

No two rooms are the same: the houses have grown over time, and you notice it in the floor plans, the ceiling heights and the sloping roofs. What every room shares is light fabric, wood, a good bed and a bathroom with everything you want after a day by the water.

Prices depend on the season, the house and the length of stay. Send us your dates and you will get a concrete offer — enquire without obligation.

Category I

Comfort room

The quiet double for two: a view into the garden or onto the dunes, an armchair by the window, a bathroom with a shower. Robe and sauna towel are ready — the walk to the spa is short.

  • 2 guests
  • Double bed
  • Garden or dune view

Dune-view room

Category III

Captain's suite

Right at the top, under the reed: sloping beams above the bed, a roof window onto the night sky and a living area of its own. These are the rooms guests book in winter and come back to in summer.

  • 2–3 guests
  • Sleeping and living area
  • Roof window above the bed

Category IV

Holiday apartment

Living for a while rather than staying a few nights: own kitchen, living area, terrace. For families, for long stays, for everyone who likes to make their own coffee in the morning — and still wants to sit in the restaurant at night.

  • 2–4 guests
  • Own kitchen
  • Terrace or balcony
