The ensemble

Four houses, one house

Two hotels, two guest houses and a house of holiday apartments — around fifty units in all, every one of them within walking distance of the others and of the beach.

The houses stand on and behind Dorfstraße, each with its own character and its own garden. What they share is the reed on the roof, the short walk to the sea — and the fact that the restaurant, the breakfast garden, the pool and both spa areas are open to every guest, no matter which house holds the key.

House I

Haus Namenlos

The original house on Dorfstraße, and the one the whole ensemble is named after. Reception, the restaurant and the sea terrace are here — for most guests this is where the day begins and ends.

  • Reception, restaurant, café and sea terrace
  • Spa area with steam bath, solarium and treatment rooms
  • Rooms and suites, many with a balcony on the quiet side
House II

Haus Fischerwiege

The sea-blue house sits a little further back, quietly in the green. Guests who choose it are usually after exactly that: less coming and going, more garden, and the pool in their own house.

  • Indoor pool with a tall window front into the green
  • Saunas and quiet areas
  • Rooms and suites, many with a terrace onto the garden
House III

The guest houses

Two smaller houses with only a handful of rooms each — quiet, familiar, and for anyone who would like things a little more private still. Breakfast, the restaurant and the spa areas are a few steps away in the main houses.

  • Only a few rooms per house, and quiet to match
  • Their own garden with places to sit
  • Full access to everything in the ensemble
House IV

The holiday apartments

Apartments with their own kitchen and their own terrace, for longer stays and for families. You can cook for yourself — or simply walk across in the morning and sit down at the breakfast table.

  • Own kitchen, living area and terrace or balcony
  • Breakfast and half board can be added
  • Particularly suited to stays of a week or more
Shared

What belongs to all the houses

The key works everywhere: staying in one house means using the others as a matter of course.

Restaurant & sea terrace

An evening menu of regional cooking, served outdoors towards the water in fine weather.

Breakfast garden & café

The breakfast buffet, and in the afternoon cake from our own kitchen.

Pool & saunas

Both spa areas are open to the guests of all four houses.

Underground parking

Spaces at the house, so the car can stay put for the whole stay.

Wi-Fi throughout

Free in every house — though we rather hope you will not need much of it.

The way to the beach

Around fifty metres to the dune crossing, on foot from any of the houses.
