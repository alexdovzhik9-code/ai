Four houses under thatch, fifty metres from the sea

Between the Baltic and the lagoon, in the middle of the artists' village of Ahrenshoop: an ensemble of hotels, guest houses and apartments — with a restaurant, a sea terrace, an indoor pool and two spa areas open to everyone who stays here.

Welcome

A place that takes its time

Thatched roofs, dune sand at the door, the sound of the Baltic behind the dyke: Haus Namenlos has stood on Dorfstraße for generations — today alongside the sea-blue Haus Fischerwiege, two guest houses and a house of holiday apartments.

The houses stand a few steps apart and share everything that makes the stay: restaurant and sea terrace, the breakfast garden, the pool, the saunas and the quiet rooms. Whichever house holds your key, the others are yours too.

Dining

First the evening, then the sea

The kitchen cooks regionally and by the season: fish from the Baltic, game and mushrooms from the Darß forest, vegetables from the farms of the Fischland.

In fine weather we serve on the sea terrace, and in the afternoon the café has cake from our own kitchen. The morning starts with a generous breakfast buffet — in the breakfast garden or, if you prefer, in your room.

Spa

Two areas, one key

Haus Fischerwiege holds the pool with its view into the green, along with saunas and quiet areas. Haus Namenlos has the steam bath, the solarium, a tub for two under a starry sky, and the treatment rooms.

  • Both areas are open to guests of all the houses
  • Robe and sauna towel are waiting in your room
  • Massages and treatments by appointment

Ahrenshoop

The place people come back for

Ahrenshoop lies on the narrow spit between the Baltic Sea and the Saaler Bodden. More than 130 years ago painters founded an artists' colony here — galleries, studios and the art museum still tell that story.
